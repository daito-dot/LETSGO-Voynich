## Active frontier — selected-pair occupancy specificity

Current retained result:

> slot3 and slot5 are almost mutually exclusive occupancy channels, and this relation transfers across physical leaves beyond line-local marginal frequencies.

The next experiment must not treat that same pair as an unseen discovery. It was selected by the E10→#55 path.

### Phase / Issue 55C target

Primary question:

> Is the slot3×slot5 exclusion unusually strong or structurally distinctive relative to the complete 12-slot occupancy-dependence graph, after selection and multiplicity are accounted for?

Minimum design requirements:

1. freeze the 12-slot binary occupancy representation before target scoring;
2. compute all slot-pair dependence under the same physical-leaf cross-fit;
3. include the fact that slot3×slot5 was previously selected when defining the confirmatory target;
4. use line-local or token-structure-preserving nulls that do not destroy trivial slot occupancy rates;
5. report the whole pairwise landscape, not only the selected pair;
6. predeclare any exceptional-pair criterion and multiplicity correction;
7. separately test whether the selected exclusion remains stable across register/Currier/token-position strata;
8. where technically defensible, replicate with an independent transcription/representation without mapping by target outcome.

### Interpretation branches

If slot3×slot5 is exceptional after the above controls:

- retain it as a specific morphotactic incompatibility;
- next ask whether it constrains a reversible surface-transform grammar.

If many slot pairs show comparable exclusions:

- model the full occupancy graph as token-construction grammar;
- do not privilege slot3×slot5 because it happened to emerge from E10.

If the effect collapses under stratification or independent representation:

- narrow the result to the relevant register/representation and do not generalize manuscript-wide.

## Parallel source lane — real historical ciphertext

The Phase72 source-development branch is not accepted current science and should not be merged wholesale into main.

The useful unresolved question is external-data availability:

> Can a public corpus provide real historical ciphertext at the level of independently established messages/documents, with boundaries suitable for an entry-effect control?

Before any Voynich score:

1. fix source authority and license/availability;
2. fix message/document identities and boundary semantics externally;
3. require at least two genuinely independent historical-practice source collections if that remains the intended comparison claim;
4. freeze preprocessing and the exact comparison statistic;
5. only then authorize S1 or other Voynich comparison.

Do not substitute physical page starts for message starts after seeing source metadata.

### Direct music

Issue #26 remains closed. Reopen a music-specific interpretation only if a new constraint exists before target scoring: an auditable exact historical key, independent manuscript-local selector, prospectively finite historical family, or new source with a genuinely new consequence.

## Repository-maintenance rules

Before starting new science:

1. current `main` is the descriptive source of truth;
2. check open PRs and active research branches;
3. integrate completed first-reveal work into main through a current-main branch rather than merging highly diverged historical branches wholesale;
4. ensure `research/STATUS.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `RESUME.md` agree on the frontier;
5. preserve negative results, raw-result hashes and first-reveal provenance;
6. close completed research Issues after their authoritative integration is on main.
