# Phase 64 strategic decision — freeze before autonomy results

Status: **frozen before any Phase64 scientific outcome**.

Phase63B has closed the independent-transcription gate positively. The next task is therefore not to repair A1 residuals and not to promote a semantic conclusion. It is to attack the largest remaining direct explanatory dependency of the current leading structural mechanism.

## 1. Current evidence before Phase64

The A1 mechanism has survived, in sequence:

1. a held-out exposed structural gate;
2. a common N0/C0/A1 tournament;
3. a sealed prospective recurrence-distance prediction;
4. removal of token types unique to held-out leaves;
5. independent v101/EvaT transcription replication and frozen-parameter transfer.

The strongest remaining direct target-dependence is now:

> **A1-R1 is still handed the empirical training-side token inventory and its edit1 topology as its output vocabulary.**

This matters because the local-family mechanism can only mutate among neighbors that already exist in that empirical inventory.

## 2. Phase64 next-frontier choice

Three strategic directions were considered before any new result:

- **G autonomy** — remove the empirical token inventory from A1 output generation;
- **C1 fairness** — build a materially stronger but bounded meaningful-text + encoding competitor;
- **content bridge** — independently map text units to external objects/content and demand unseen content prediction.

The selection criteria are:

1. ability to falsify the current leading explanation;
2. reduction in target dependence or family-comparison unfairness;
3. held-out/prospective executability;
4. low freedom to repair after seeing outcomes;
5. feasibility without inventing an external semantic mapping.

### Frozen choice

**Phase64A will test G autonomy first.**

Reason: it removes the largest remaining direct dependency of A1 using data and scoring infrastructure that already exist, while requiring no new residual-fitting mechanism.

This is a challenge to A1, not an attempt to improve it.

## 3. C1 fairness remains binding debt

A successful Phase64A does **not** authorize the claim `G > C` or `G > N` at the family level.

C0 was intentionally simple: global, reversible, boundary-blind and low-complexity. A developed G candidate cannot establish family dominance by beating C0 alone.

A separate Phase64/65 C1 track must therefore receive a serious, independently motivated design with:

- meaningful plaintext source(s) fixed independently of Voynich outcomes;
- historically or mathematically motivated encoding operations;
- no Voynich-specific paragraph/section tuning unless explicitly charged as model complexity;
- held-out model selection only;
- explicit complexity accounting;
- prospective diagnostics not chosen after C1 output is seen.

## 4. Content bridge remains essential but not fabricated

Structural evidence still cannot answer whether the formal layer carries, transforms or coexists with semantic content.

Content promotion still requires independently grounded object/paragraph mapping and unseen prediction. Current localized mapping is not sufficiently established to justify inventing labels or tuning a mapping against Voynich text.

Therefore content work remains essential but is not used as the immediate executable gate.

## 5. No-A2 rule for Phase64A

Phase64A may change **only the source of the output token inventory**.

The following are forbidden in Phase64A:

- adding a second local process;
- extending direct memory beyond 10;
- adding explicit long-range B4/B5 machinery;
- adding section-specific or fold-specific grammar;
- adding a line-position repair rule;
- changing Phase61C entry-strength/local-family probabilities;
- changing H62-P1 bins/nulls/diagnostics;
- selecting a morphology generator using held-out S1/S2/S3/H62 results.

Any such extension would be a new charged architecture (A2 or later), not a robustness ablation.

## 6. Interpretation freeze

If Phase64A passes, the allowed promotion is narrow:

> A1's tested structural advantage does not require an explicit empirical training-token inventory as its output vocabulary under the frozen lower-level morphology generator.

If it fails, record which component fails before changing the morphology model or A1:

> The current A1 advantage materially depends on empirical inventory topology under the tested autonomous morphology replacement.

A Phase64A failure does not falsify every possible autonomous G mechanism. A pass does not establish semantic emptiness, historical identity, or decipherment.
