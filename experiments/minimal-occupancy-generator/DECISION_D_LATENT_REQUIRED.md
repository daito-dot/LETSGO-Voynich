## What is now ruled out as sufficient

Under the tested model family, the complete replicated R1 topology is not sufficiently generated by:

1. slot prevalence;
2. occupied count K;
3. coarse K/R/S geometry;
4. K/R/S plus position-specific nearest-neighbor transitions;
5. all of the above plus generic nonadjacent coupling determined only by slot separation.

The last extension does carry some signal: paired median `T_M4-T_M3=+0.021217706030355665`. The failure therefore does not show that nonlocal structure is absent. It shows that **distance alone is too coarse a representation of the remaining nonlocal structure**.

## Licensed next hypothesis

A separately preregistered compact latent token-construction model is licensed.

> a small hidden construction state changes several slot/transition preferences jointly; mixing a small number of such states induces configuration-specific long-range dependencies without directly fitting distant target edges.
