# Issue #75 Phase A — minimal 12-slot occupancy generator

Status: **PREREGISTERED BEFORE EXECUTABLE / NO PHASE-A TARGET RESULT**

Parent frontier: Issue #75.

## 1. Scientific object

This phase operates directly on the frozen 12-slot representation of one space-delimited Voynich token.

For every parser-accepted token, the established representation is a binary vector

`x in {0,1}^12`,

where `x_s=1` means slot `s` is occupied.

The confirmatory target is the already frozen **complete 66-edge null-residual dependency topology** over the `C(12,2)=66` unordered slot pairs.

This phase does not model literal EVA glyph values or token spellings. It asks how much of the replicated occupancy topology can be generated from deliberately lower-order occupancy statistics.

## 3. Primary question

> **Are independent slot marginals, or independent slot main effects plus the distribution of occupied-slot count K, sufficient to generate the replicated R1 occupancy topology?**

The phase deliberately tests only the first low-order fork. It does not introduce latent states, pairwise interaction parameters, selected edges, or target-aware repair.

## 5. Cross-fitted generation design

The full generated corpus is built by five-fold cross-fitting.

For each held-out physical fold `f`:

1. fit the family only to occupancy signatures from the other four ZL3b folds;
2. use no occupancy bit from fold `f` when fitting that fold's generator;
3. generate exactly the same number of occupancy signatures as the parser-accepted target token slots in fold `f`;
4. assign them in deterministic generated order to the exact accepted-token line skeleton of fold `f`.

The line skeleton contributes only:

- number of generated occupancy-token slots per line;
- physical fold membership;
- deterministic line order.

The held-out line's observed 12-bit occupancies, slot marginals, K distribution, pair statistics, Q values or residual graph are not supplied to the generator.

After all five held-out folds are generated, they are concatenated into one cross-fitted synthetic corpus of exactly `25,071` occupancy signatures.

Thus every synthetic token is generated by a model that did not train on the physical fold occupying that token slot.

## 6. Model families

Exactly three scientific families are licensed in Phase A, plus a second independent bank of the positive control for calibration.

### M0 — non-empty independent-slot maximum-entropy model

Scientific responsibility:

> Are the 12 training-only slot marginals themselves sufficient?

For each training split, fit the maximum-entropy distribution over all `2^12 - 1 = 4095` non-empty occupancy signatures:

`P_M0(x) proportional to exp(sum_s lambda_s x_s)` for `x != 0`.

The 12 main-effect parameters are fit so that model expected slot occupancies match the 12 training-only empirical slot occupancies, within a frozen numerical convergence tolerance.

No K-distribution constraint and no pair/edge interaction parameter is present.

Complexity report:

- 12 fitted main-effect parameters per cross-fit training split;
- no pairwise parameters;
- no target-edge fitting.

### M1 — training K distribution + conditional slot main effects

Scientific responsibility:

> If M0 is insufficient, does adding only the token's occupied-slot-count distribution explain the residual topology?

For each training split:

1. freeze empirical training-only `q_k = P(K=k)` for `k=1..12`;
2. conditional on sampled `K=k`, draw a signature from

`P_M1(x | K=k) proportional to exp(sum_s lambda_s x_s)`

among signatures with exactly `k` occupied slots.

The conditional model contains no slot-pair interaction terms.

Because adding a common constant to all `lambda_s` cancels conditional on K, fix `lambda_0 = 0` and fit the remaining 11 main-effect parameters.

The empirical K distribution contributes at most 11 independent probabilities. Report both components separately rather than collapsing them into one nominal parameter count.

### M+ — empirical occupancy-signature resampling positive control

Scientific responsibility:

> Can the cross-fitted training inventory itself regenerate the held-out/full replicated occupancy topology under this evaluation interface?

For each held-out fold, sample complete 12-bit signatures with replacement from the other four training folds.

This model supplies the empirical pattern inventory and is **not a mechanistic explanation**.

Two independent, identically specified banks are frozen:

- `MPLUS-A`;
- `MPLUS-B`.

They differ only in deterministic seed namespace. Their target-score difference provides an empirical positive-control stochastic variation scale.

No other Phase-A family may be added after target reveal.

## 7. Deterministic fit and sampling

### State space

Enumerate the 4095 non-empty binary signatures in ascending integer-mask order `1..4095`, with bit `s` corresponding to slot `s`.

### Parameter fitting

M0 and M1 must use an explicit deterministic convex exponential-family solver implemented in the Phase-A executable.

Required properties:

- no random initialization;
- start all free `lambda` at zero;
- exact state-space expectation and covariance, not Monte Carlo fitting;
- deterministic Newton or equivalent convex updates;
- frozen line search / convergence rule;
- maximum absolute fitted marginal error must be reported for every fold;
- any fit failing the frozen tolerance is a failed case, not licensed for manual repair.

The executable may depend on frozen NumPy/SciPy versions for matrix operations, but may not use target-edge loss as an optimizer objective.

### Randomness

Use SHA-256-derived deterministic seeds.

Generation namespaces are exactly:

- `issue75:phaseA:M0:rep{r}:fold{f}:generate`
- `issue75:phaseA:M1:rep{r}:fold{f}:generate`
- `issue75:phaseA:MPLUS-A:rep{r}:fold{f}:generate`
- `issue75:phaseA:MPLUS-B:rep{r}:fold{f}:generate`

with `r=0..30`, `f=0..4`.

No rerolls are permitted.

## 12. Positive-control calibration and family decision

No arbitrary round R threshold is used.

### 12.1 M+ calibration validity

The historically frozen pooled within-reading residual reliabilities are:

- ZL3b `W_ALL = 0.9447148364`;
- IT2a `W_ALL = 0.9537725542`.

Define the prospective positive-control floor:

`T_control_floor = min(0.9447148364, 0.9537725542) = 0.9447148364`.

Threshold source: **T2**, target internal-replication variation already frozen before Issue #75.

M+ calibration is valid only if:

- median `T` of MPLUS-A >= `T_control_floor`; and
- median `T` of MPLUS-B >= `T_control_floor`.

If either fails:

> `POSITIVE_CONTROL_CALIBRATION_FAILED_STOP`

No M0/M1 sufficiency class may be promoted. The phase stops for a separately preregistered criterion/interface audit.

### 12.2 Positive-control stochastic tolerance

For each paired replicate `r`, define:

`T_plus_center[r] = (T_MPLUS_A[r] + T_MPLUS_B[r]) / 2`

and

`D_plus_self[r] = abs(T_MPLUS_A[r] - T_MPLUS_B[r])`.

Freeze

`delta_plus = empirical 95th percentile of D_plus_self[0..30]`.

Threshold source: **T2**, positive-control stochastic variation generated under two independent banks with the same scientific model.

### 12.3 M0/M1 material-loss statistic

For `M in {M0,M1}` define

`gap_M = median_r( T_M[r] - T_plus_center[r] )`.

Family M has no material loss relative to empirical-inventory resampling if:

`gap_M >= -delta_plus`.

Otherwise it has material loss.

The continuous gap, all replicate T values, reading-specific R values and `delta_plus` are reported regardless of category.

### 12.4 Ordered primary classification

Provided M+ calibration is valid:

1. if M0 has no material loss:
   - `M0_INDEPENDENT_SLOT_MARGINALS_SUFFICIENT`;
2. else if M1 has no material loss:
   - `M1_MARGINALS_PLUS_OCCUPANCY_COUNT_SUFFICIENT`;
3. else:
   - `LOW_ORDER_MODELS_INSUFFICIENT_EMPIRICAL_PATTERN_STRUCTURE_REQUIRED`.

This is an ordered minimality result. A richer family cannot erase a simpler family's success.

M2/M3 are not licensed within this first reveal.

## 14. Interpretation map

### M0 succeeds

Licensed claim:

> Under this cross-fitted evaluation, the replicated R1 topology is recoverable from training-only independent slot marginals plus the logical non-empty-token constraint.

Consequence:

- R1 should be sharply narrowed as a higher-order mechanism discriminator;
- investigate why candidate-owned residualization permits the apparent topology before adding richer mechanism families.

### M0 fails, M1 succeeds

Licensed claim:

> Training-only slot main effects plus occupied-slot-count distribution are sufficient; explicit empirical occupancy-pattern inventory or pair interactions are not required by R1.

Consequence:

- promote occupancy-count regulation as the minimal tested explanatory layer.

### M0 and M1 fail, M+ succeeds

Licensed claim:

> Structure in the empirical occupancy-pattern inventory beyond slot marginals and occupied-slot count is required under this test, but no compact generative explanation has yet been established.

Only this outcome licenses a new preregistered M2/M3 phase.

### M+ fails

No scientific conclusion about M0/M1 sufficiency may be promoted. Audit the cross-fitting/evaluation interface under a new preregistration.
