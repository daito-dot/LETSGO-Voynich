# Phase 69A — meaningful plaintext + context-adaptive homophones

Status: **CLOSED — C2-LH1 LOCALITY-ONLY PARTIAL**

## Question

Can meaningful plaintext remain reversibly encoded while a deliberate context-sensitive homophone choice creates the strongest short-range structural signature currently known in Voynichese?

The fixed base system was the published Naibbe v2 homophonic cipher applied to the same four medieval Latin plaintext manuscripts used in the earlier N/C/G tournament. The new mechanism was deliberately narrow:

- `LH0`: choose a valid homophone using the published candidate weights, with no recent-ciphertext preference;
- `LH1`: if any valid homophone is exactly one edit away from one of the preceding ten raw ciphertext tokens, choose from that local subset; otherwise use the normal weighted candidate set.

Plaintext segmentation and output-space decisions were paired between LH0/LH1, so the difference isolates the recent-ciphertext homophone selector.

The recent-context edit1 rule is **not historically attested**. It was introduced as a new, complexity-charged mechanism after the published Naibbe model had failed the Voynich locality signature. The result is therefore developmental mechanism evidence, not independent historical confirmation.

## Reversibility and mechanism use

The raw cipher remained fully reversible:

- plaintext units over 4 manuscripts × 5 realisations: **172,958**
- LH0 exact raw decode accuracy: **1.000**
- LH1 exact raw decode accuracy: **1.000**

LH1 actually had an opportunity to use the local rule on **94,100 / 172,958 = 54.41%** of plaintext units. Every such opportunity selected an edit1-local homophone, as preregistered.

So this was not a mostly inactive rule.

## Paired control — LH0

Without the local rule, the weighted homophonic cipher remains far from the main Voynich entry/locality structure:

| metric | LH0 / Voynich |
|---|---:|
| S1 paragraph-entry projection | -0.056× |
| S2 previous-10 near-family excess | -0.042× |
| S3 aggregate line-position eta2 | 0.526× |

H62-P1:

- mean `D_profile`: **1.599**
- mean `|ΔC_short|`: **0.984**

This is the expected control: homophony by itself does not generate the Voynich short-range effect.

## Adaptive local selector — LH1

The one-edit recent-context selector changes the structure dramatically:

| metric | LH1 / Voynich |
|---|---:|
| S1 paragraph-entry projection | **0.113×** |
| S2 previous-10 near-family excess | **6.214×** |
| S3 aggregate line-position eta2 | **0.568×** |

H62-P1:

- mean `D_profile`: **0.689**
- median `D_profile`: **0.783**
- mean `|ΔC_short|`: **0.249**

The preregistered causal mechanism check passed all three requirements relative to paired LH0:

1. S2 increased;
2. H62 `|ΔC_short|` error decreased;
3. H62 `D_profile` decreased.

LH1 also beats N0 and C0 in **5/5 folds on both H62 diagnostics**.

## Comparison with A1-R1

This is the most informative part of the result.

The frozen A1-R1 reference has approximately:

- mean H62 `D_profile`: **0.767**
- median H62 `D_profile`: **0.809**
- mean `|ΔC_short|`: **0.118**

LH1 has:

- mean H62 `D_profile`: **0.689**
- median H62 `D_profile`: **0.783**
- mean `|ΔC_short|`: **0.249**

So the meaningful-text LH1 construction is actually **closer than A1-R1 on mean and median five-bin recurrence-profile shape**, and wins `D_profile` against A1-R1 in 3/5 folds.

But it is worse on short-range concentration magnitude:

- A1-R1 mean C-short error: about **0.118**
- LH1 mean C-short error: **0.249**
- LH1 wins that metric in only 2/5 folds.

More importantly, the exposed scalar score shows why LH1 is not a full explanation:

- its S2 is about **6.21 times** the Voynich value;
- its S1 paragraph-entry effect is only about **0.11 times** Voynich.

It creates too much locality while still lacking the entry system.

Therefore:

- H62 viability against N0/C0: **pass**
- full exposed S1/S2/S3 gate: **fail**
- structurally viable: **false**
- A1-R1 rival: **false**

## Decision

Frozen classification:

> **C2-LH1 LOCALITY-ONLY PARTIAL**

The main scientific consequence is not that this cipher is a Voynich solution. It is that the strongest short-range Voynich recurrence geometry is **compatible with fully reversible meaningful plaintext** under a bounded context-dependent surface encoder.

That matters for mechanism interpretation. A1-like recurrence can no longer reasonably be treated, by itself, as evidence that the text must be semantic-free or generated by self-copying gibberish. A meaningful plaintext can sit underneath and still produce an H62 recurrence profile at least as close as A1 on one major profile diagnostic.

At the same time, this specific adaptive cipher is wrong as a complete model. It overshoots locality badly and does not produce the paragraph-entry register. The result identifies a separation of responsibilities:

- a recent-context homophonic surface rule is sufficient to create strong short-range recurrence;
- the Voynich paragraph-entry phenomenon requires something else;
- the observed locality strength requires tighter control than the all-or-nothing LH1 rule.

## Blinded routing constraint

`DECISION_A_BLIND.md` was committed while the first scientific run was still in progress and before any Phase69 score was visible.

That routing decision forbids post-result tuning of:

- previous-history length;
- edit-distance definition;
- a soft similarity threshold;
- local-selection strength.

Therefore we will **not** tune LH1 from 6.21× down toward the Voynich S2 target.

A further model may add a separately frozen entry mechanism only as a new model-family step, or may explicitly test whether the already-supported A1 formal layer can coexist reversibly with meaningful plaintext. That would be a semantic-compatibility construction, not evidence that the resulting composite is the historical cipher.

## Historical boundary

Historical evidence supports the ingredients only at a coarser level. Fifteenth-century northern Italian diplomatic ciphers used multiple substitution options/homophones, and surviving cipher instructions from the fifteenth century onward discuss diversifying homophones. Published Naibbe likewise builds a verbose reversible homophonic system from historically available materials.

No source currently establishes the exact LH1 choice rule based on edit-distance similarity to recent ciphertext. That part remains an experimental modern mechanism.
