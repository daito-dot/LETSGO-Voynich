## Current interpretation

Issue26 does **not** support the claim that visible Voynich running text is a literal Guidonian musical sequence.

The robust positive object is narrower:

> Under the adopted Zattera slot decomposition, Voynich contains a six-state morphological/dependency factor. A Guidonian 20×6 admissibility table can fit this static factor well under some comparison families.

Two different cautions are now established and should not be conflated:

1. **E3–E6 mathematical controls:** the static Guidonian fit is not uniquely required; structurally matched constructed alternatives can reproduce it. This shows mathematical non-uniqueness, but does not prove that such structures are common among real historical systems.
2. **E7 theory-internal prediction:** when the static fit is treated as the actual historical Guidonian system, its previously unused sequence consequences do not appear in held-out Voynich order.

E7 is therefore the cleaner falsification of the literal musical reading because it does not depend on claiming that arbitrary six-state systems are common.

## E7 prospective result

- the 42 allowed cells decompose into seven overlapping `ut-re-mi-fa-sol-la` hexachords;

Thus the static resemblance does not forecast the historical dynamics that would come for free if the cells were functioning as Guidonian pitches/voces.
