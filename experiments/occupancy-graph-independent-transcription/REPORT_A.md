## 14. What #58D does not establish

Do not promote #58D to any of the following:

- visible spaces are proven natural-language words;
- the 12-slot representation is the unique or historical parsing of Voynichese;
- sentence-level syntax has been identified;
- slots have semantic meanings;
- the graph is a plaintext alphabet or cipher table;
- one particular historical cipher/generator is identified;
- meaningful plaintext exists or does not exist;
- the manuscript is deciphered.

The strongest remaining representation caveat is that IT2a and ZL3b share an EVA/IVTFF framework. A genuinely different alphabet such as GC2a/v101 would require a separately preregistered coarser/invariant mapping before it can fairly challenge the same object.

That is a useful robustness lane, but after this strong reading-lineage replication it is no longer the highest-information immediate frontier.
