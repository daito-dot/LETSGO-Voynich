# Issue #186 — post-Gate0 decision

Date: 2026-09-08  
Status: **STOP CREMMA STAGE1 — NO NEW IDENTIFIABLE EXTERNAL POPULATION**

## Decision

Do not run the proposed CREMMA genre-stratified S1 Stage1.

Gate0 is technically valid, but the unchanged historical S1 representation is structurally composable on only five of the 21 externally frozen CREMMA manuscripts. Those five are exactly the manuscripts whose S1 outcomes were already visible before Issue #186: `H318`, `CLM13027`, `BIS-193`, `Mazarine915`, and `UBL758`.

A one-shot Stage1 would therefore reveal no new manuscript S1 value. It would repackage known outcomes under Type labels and would not provide the prospective external mechanism attribution requested by the near-term roadmap.

## Why no representation repair is allowed

The following repairs are explicitly rejected in this lane:

- treating page or file starts as paragraph starts;
- converting headings or drop capitals into paragraph boundaries;
- inventing boundaries from line spacing or line length;
- changing the historical line-0/line-2 S1 offset;
- lowering the historical line/token eligibility requirements;
- selecting only the CREMMA subfolders that happen to expose compatible markup.

Any of these would change the scientific representation after Gate0 showed where support exists.

## What Gate0 established

The broad CREMMA registry cannot answer the planned class-attribution question under the exact historical S1 replay:

- Medic.: 2/5 composable, both already revealed;
- Schol.: 2/5 composable, both already revealed;
- Lit.: 0/6 composable;
- Eccl.: 1/3 composable, already revealed;
- Gramm.: 0/2 composable.

This is a support/identifiability result, not an S1 null result for the non-composable manuscripts.

## Next Priority-1 admission gate

A replacement external corpus must be selected without looking at Voynich-like S1 behavior and must expose, from source authority before scoring:

1. explicit paragraph, message, record, or item boundaries with a defensible semantic/production meaning;
2. physical lineation within each item;
3. enough items with at least three retained lines and at least five tokens on the historical line-0 and line-2 positions;
4. token boundaries that can be mapped without target-driven segmentation;
5. multiple independent documents/items so replication is not a pooled-only effect;
6. a frozen mechanism/architecture label independent of S1;
7. no need to invent boundary recovery after the support audit.

Priority should be given to architecture classes still required by the roadmap: formula/recipe/list records, administrative/tabular records where the historical S1 statistic is definitionally composable, and real historical ciphertext with preserved message/paragraph boundaries. Ordinary prose already has wrong-sign historical controls in the existing CREMMA subset, and the frozen Alberti message-reset construction is also wrong-sign.

The next experiment should begin with a score-free composability audit on the new external authority. Only a corpus that passes that gate should receive the frozen S1 reveal.
