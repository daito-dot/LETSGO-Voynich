## 1. Why the frontier changes here

Through Phase64, the leading A1 structural mechanism has survived a sealed recurrence prediction, held-out-vocabulary restriction and independent-transcription transfer. Phase64A shows that its core H62 geometry is robust even after replacing the empirical token inventory, although the strict ZL autonomy gate fails on S3. Phase64B gives C a serious independently published competitor: exact no-reuse Naibbe produces a genuine partial structural resemblance but fails the paragraph-entry/local-prev10 signature and does not rival A1 on H62-P1.

Continuing to repair A1 or to search ciphers until one fits would now increase model-selection freedom faster than information gain.

The next frontier therefore moves one step up the epistemic ladder:

> **Mechanism → Content relation**

This is not decipherment and not a search for a pleasing translation.

## 2. Phase65A question

> Does the manuscript provide at least one localized content anchor whose mapping can be fixed from physical layout, image content, catalog evidence or independent scholarship without using Voynichese textual similarity, and whose population is large/repeated enough to support a preregistered unseen-item test?

Phase65A is an **anchor audit only**. It may inspect manuscript images, layout, catalog descriptions and external scholarship. It must not score a new Voynichese semantic/content statistic.

## 3. Preferred anchor families, in order

1. **Physical object ↔ text attachment** — a visually identifiable drawing/diagram/object has an auditable attached label or paragraph by layout alone. Visual descriptors may be extracted without reading the Voynichese string. Specific historical naming is not required.
2. **Independently identifiable diagram/object identity** — e.g. a diagram whose depicted identity is externally clear and whose attached text unit is localizable, provided repeated/held-out testing is feasible.
3. **Scholarly named identification** — allowed only when the identification is fixed independently of Voynichese and has enough independent support and localization to survive the hard gates below.

Disputed plant-name guesses, text-selected matches, broad section labels and retrospective semantic stories are not primary anchors.

## 4. No-result-driven repair rule

If no candidate passes Phase65A, record the content lane as externally blocked. Do not lower localization, independence or holdout requirements after seeing candidate weaknesses.

The fallback is one separately frozen historically motivated residual C hypothesis with a distinct prediction. It may not add locality/reuse merely because Phase64B showed that Naibbe lacks them.

## 5. Claim limits

A Phase65A pass means only that a defensible content-relation experiment can be preregistered. It does not mean that content is detected.

A Phase65A block means only that current external anchors are insufficient for a clean test. It does not mean that Voynichese lacks semantic content.
