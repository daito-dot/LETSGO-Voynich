# Token-construction program — purpose, object, and decision path

Status: normative orientation for the current token-construction research lane.

This document exists so that a future researcher or agent can recover **why this lane is being run** before reading individual statistics. Phase-specific frozen plans, first-reveal artifacts, and reports remain authoritative for exact methods and numbers.

## One-sentence purpose

> Determine whether Voynich **space-delimited tokens** are generated by a constrained internal construction system, whether the non-trivial part of that system is shared across the manuscript, and which of those constraints are robust enough to become prospective restrictions on later inverse/decoding models.

## What the object is

The object is **not sentence grammar** and it is not yet a claim about natural-language words.

We use `space-delimited token` deliberately because the manuscript's visible spaces have not been established as natural-language word boundaries.

For the current representation, a parsed token is described through a frozen 12-slot construction. Each slot can be occupied or empty. The lane asks about dependencies among those occupancy decisions:

- which slots tend to co-occur;
- which slots tend to exclude one another;
- which dependencies remain after controlling for simpler occupancy structure;
- whether the residual dependency system is shared across manuscript strata;
- whether that system survives an independent transcription/reading rather than being an artifact of one representation.

A relation such as `slot3 occupied -> slot5 unusually absent` is therefore a **token-internal construction constraint**. It is not, by itself, a meaning assignment, a plaintext letter relation, or a cipher table.

## Why this matters to decipherment

The immediate statistical target is narrow, but the scientific reason is broader.

A later decipherment model should not be allowed to explain arbitrary-looking token forms after the fact. It should prospectively reproduce the token-internal constraints that survive independent controls and readings.

This lane is partly answering two prerequisite questions:

> **How many token-generation systems are we trying to invert?**

and, after #58C,

> **Which token-internal constraints are manuscript-real enough to constrain that inverse problem?**

This still does not establish whether the underlying information is natural language, ciphered text, artificial text, or another structured source.

## What has been established so far

### Issue #55

A selected slot3×slot5 relation was real, but #55B showed that essentially all of the measured dependence reduced to the binary fact that the two slots are almost never occupied together.

This established a genuine token-internal occupancy constraint, not a semantic interpretation.

### Issue #58A

The selection-aware complete-graph audit tested all `C(12,2)=66` slot pairs rather than privileging slot3×slot5.

Frozen result:

> `BROAD OCCUPANCY GRAMMAR; SLOT3xSLOT5 NOT UNIQUE`

Twenty-two edges qualified under the frozen family-wise primary rule. The graph contains both strong co-occurrence and strong exclusion. The research object therefore moved from one selected pair to the **complete signed occupancy graph**.

### Issue #58B

#58B asked whether the complete signed graph is shared across externally defined Currier/section/line-position strata.

Observed full-graph similarities were numerically high, with strong within-stratum reliability and generally strong cross-stratum transfer. However, the frozen line-local marginal-preserving null itself produced extremely high complete-graph correlations (`median maxT ≈ 0.949`). None of the seven planned similarities was family-wise exceptional.

Frozen classifications:

> `CURRIER/SECTION GRAPH STABILITY INCONCLUSIVE`

> `LINE-POSITION GRAPH STABILITY INCONCLUSIVE`

This did **not** show that the strata differ. It showed that raw complete-graph similarity is strongly contaminated by lower-order slot prevalence / line-local occupancy architecture.

### Issue #58C / #64

#58C directly addressed that ambiguity by calibrating every one of the 66 conditional edges against its own line-local occupancy-null distribution.

Crucially, the design separated:

- `1,000` deterministic **reference nulls** used only to define the residual transform; and
- an independent `1,000` **test nulls** used only for confirmatory significance and multiplicity control.

The first gate asked whether a residual graph exists at all before cross-stratum similarity could be interpreted.

Frozen first-reveal result:

> **`RESIDUAL GRAPH EXISTS WITH STRATUM MODULATION`**

Pooled result:

- real residual energy `E_ALL = 3.23155`;
- independent test-null median `0.99004`;
- independent test-null maximum `1.28318`;
- `p_exist_ALL = 1/1001`;
- physical-leaf residual reliability `W_ALL = 0.94471`.

All seven planned strata independently passed the family-wise residual-existence gate with `p_E,maxT = 1/1001` and high fold reliability.

All seven planned cross-stratum residual similarities also exceeded the complete independent test-null maxT ensemble (`p_R,maxT = 1/1001` for each), but exact invariance did not hold.

Frozen family results:

> **`REGISTER/SECTION RESIDUAL MODULATION`**

> **`LINE-POSITION RESIDUAL MODULATION`**

Stable residual relations included the three Currier-B section comparisons and line interior-vs-final. Currier A-vs-B within Herbal and comparisons involving line-initial tokens were related but modulated. No contrast met the frozen `DIFFERENT_RESIDUAL_OR_MIXTURE` gate.

## Current working interpretation

The lower-order explanation exposed by #58B is now insufficient.

Under the frozen 12-slot representation:

> **Voynich space-delimited tokens contain a broad, reproducible internal interaction system that cannot be explained by line-local slot prevalence alone. A large part of that residual construction system is shared across the tested manuscript strata, with measurable Currier/section/line-position modulation rather than evidence for wholly separate grammars.**

This is the strongest current token-construction statement.

Do not shorten it to “one universal grammar.” Exact invariance was not supported.

## Current scientific uncertainty

#58C is still based on the ZL3b transcription plus the established 12-slot parsing representation.

The important remaining alternative is now **representation/transcription dependence**:

> Would the residual-existence result and the shared-core-plus-modulation geometry survive a materially independent Voynich transcription/reading without retuning the slot grammar or selecting favorable edges?

This must be answered before the residual graph is promoted into a hard constraint on reversible/inverse decoding models.

## Next scientific move

Run a plan-first independent-transcription/representation replication.

The next phase should separate at least:

1. **independent residual existence** — under the independent reading's own prospectively frozen line-local null calibration, does a non-trivial 66-edge residual graph exist?
2. **cross-reading graph replication** — does its complete residual graph agree with the frozen ZL3b #58C graph beyond a prospectively defined null/threshold?
3. **geometry replication** — do the broad shared-core and modulation conclusions survive, without requiring exact equality of every edge or stratum statistic?

Do not tune glyph normalization, slot assignment, edge selection, or thresholds after inspecting the independent target graph.

## Decision path

### Independent reading replicates residual existence and graph geometry

Promote the replicated residual constraints as manuscript-level surface-generation constraints. Then use only those replicated constraints prospectively to restrict reversible generative/inverse model families.

### Residual existence replicates but graph topology shifts materially

Retain “strong token-internal construction exists” but narrow the exact 66-edge topology to a representation-sensitive claim. Identify a representation-invariant coarser constraint before inverse-model promotion.

### Residual existence fails independently

Downgrade #58C to a ZL3b/parser-dependent structural result. Do not use its edge topology as a hard decipherment constraint.

### Replication is support-limited/inconclusive

Record the limitation and do not tune the independent transcription mapping to force agreement.

## Interpretation boundaries

This lane must not silently become any of the following:

- a proof that spaces are true word boundaries;
- sentence-level syntax analysis;
- semantic assignment to slots;
- a plaintext alphabet or cipher-table inference;
- evidence for or against all ciphers;
- evidence that the manuscript is meaningless;
- a revival of the music/Sloane interpretation that historically exposed one residual;
- decipherment.
