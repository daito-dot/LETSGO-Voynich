# Next research frontier — Issue #58B signed occupancy-graph stability

Status: next plan-first frontier after Issue #58A first reveal and integration.

## Why the frontier moves here

Issue #55A established a cross-leaf slot3×slot5 dependence. Issue #55B reduced the signal to binary occupancy exclusion. Issue #58A then removed the selection bias around that pair by scoring the complete frozen 12-slot occupancy graph under one common held-out statistic and one global maxT family.

#58A result:

- 22 of 66 slot pairs survive family-wise maxT with all five held-out folds positive;
- selected `(3,5)` ranks only 22/66;
- `(3,5)` remains a real exclusion (`p_maxT=1/1001`, phi `-0.20648`) but loses family-wise extremeness after conditioning on other-slot occupancy complexity (`p_maxT=0.55644`);
- the graph contains strong positive and negative relations, not merely exclusions;
- `(8,10)` is the strongest primary relation (`0.76767 bits/token`, phi `+0.92378`) and remains strong after `K_other` conditioning;
- all 66 canonical two-slot co-occupancies are parser-admissible.

Frozen classification:

> **`BROAD OCCUPANCY GRAMMAR; SLOT3xSLOT5 NOT UNIQUE`**

The object of study is therefore the complete signed occupancy grammar, not slot3×slot5 in isolation.

## Protocol boundary inherited from #58A

The umbrella Issue #58 required register/Currier/token-position stratification or interaction tests to be predeclared. #58A's frozen plan did not define those tests before its first reveal.

They must therefore be performed as a **new plan-first phase**. They cannot be added retrospectively to #58A and described as confirmatory.

Likewise, the #58A ranking is now observed information. A #58B plan must not select only `(8,10)`, `(8,11)`, `(10,11)` or other high-scoring edges and treat them as prospective discoveries.

## Primary next question

> Is the complete signed 12-slot occupancy graph stable across externally defined manuscript register/Currier/section and token-position strata, or is the pooled #58A graph substantially generated by mixture between subsets?

This is a structural question. It does not assign meaning to slots.

## Required source audit before preregistration

Before defining target strata, establish which metadata can be reconstructed from current authoritative sources without consulting #58A edge outcomes.

For every proposed stratum record:

1. metadata authority / source;
2. deterministic mapping rule from physical leaf / line to stratum;
3. population support before pair scoring;
4. missing / ambiguous assignments and their frozen handling;
5. whether the stratum is genuinely external to the occupancy outcomes.

Candidate dimensions may include Currier A/B, manuscript section/register, and line-relative token position, but only source-supported definitions may enter the frozen plan.

## Required #58B statistical design

The plan must be committed before any #58B target reveal and should satisfy all of the following:

1. reuse the frozen 12-slot binary representation unless a separately justified representation change is declared before scoring;
2. use all 66 edges as the primary graph object;
3. freeze one or more graph-level stability / interaction statistics before scoring;
4. avoid selecting only #58A leaders as primary targets;
5. preserve physical-leaf separation in training/evaluation;
6. control for trivial occupancy-rate differences and line-local structure;
7. freeze all strata, minimum-support rules and multiplicity correction before target scoring;
8. report the complete stratum × edge landscape even when individual relations weaken or reverse;
9. distinguish shared-core graph structure from stratum-specific modulation;
10. treat an independent-transcription/representation check as a separate non-promoting replication unless its mapping is independently fixed in advance.

## Preferred falsifiable model family

The strongest next model should compare at least these possibilities:

### H58B-CORE — shared graph

A common signed occupancy-interaction structure explains most held-out relation across externally defined strata; stratum-specific deviations are small relative to the shared component.

### H58B-MIX — mixture-generated pooled graph

A material fraction of the #58A pooled graph arises from differences in slot prevalence / construction rules between strata, and cross-stratum transfer degrades substantially.

### H58B-MOD — shared core plus modulation

A stable common graph exists, but a predeclared subset of graph-level interaction mass varies systematically with register or token position.

The plan must define numerical decision rules before reveal rather than selecting the preferred interpretation afterward.

## What each outcome means

### Shared graph survives

Promote the signed occupancy grammar to a strong manuscript-wide surface constraint. Then prioritize representation invariance and a prospectively constrained reversible construction/decoding model.

### Shared core plus modulation

Model a hierarchical grammar: common token-construction constraints plus limited register/position modulation. Do not infer semantic meanings for modulated edges without independent evidence.

### Strong mixture / instability

Do not treat the pooled #58A graph as one manuscript-wide grammar. Move to register-specific or hierarchical construction models before any transform/decoding claim.

### Independent representation fails

Keep the graph explicitly tied to the current slot representation until a more invariant structural object is found.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not:

- assign semantic meanings to any slot from graph sign or strength;
- infer a cipher table from occupancy dependence;
- revive Sloane/music because #55 originated in E10;
- choose only #58A top edges for confirmatory testing;
- choose strata after seeing their edge matrices;
- change the slot parser after seeing #58B results to improve stability;
- add an invertible-transform interpretation before graph stability is established.
