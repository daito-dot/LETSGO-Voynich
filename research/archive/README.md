# Research archive

Files in this directory are preserved for provenance and historical reconstruction. They are **not current navigation or scientific-state authority** unless a later document explicitly cites one for a historical fact.

Current entry points are:

- `../../RESUME.md`
- `../../ROADMAP.md`
- `../STATUS.md`
- `../HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md`
- `../PREDICTIVE_INFORMATION_PROGRAM.md`
- `../RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`

Naming convention:

`<DOCUMENT>_PRE_<ISSUE_OR_MILESTONE>_<YYYYMMDD>.md`

Historical phase plans/reports normally remain in their original `experiments/` locations because Issues and provenance files link to those exact paths. The archive is primarily for superseded moving documents such as status/navigation snapshots and conflicting legacy copies.
