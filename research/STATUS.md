## 11. Current compact working model

The most economical live **surface-production** description is:

1. certain visible spaces delimit bounded production/construction episodes;
2. inside each episode, a compact second-order occupied-slot construction grammar captures most topology;
3. short edit-near recurrence/cache contributes very local history;
4. within a source line, generic onset/position plus immediate previous-terminal → next-initial identity contributes a small observable adjacency effect;
5. source-line breaks reset that useful short raw context;

7. some strengths and literal mappings vary with observable Currier regime;
8. no separately frozen flexible RESET/LINE capacity is currently selected beyond the augmented observable core.

This is a compact predictive surface model, not a decipherment.
