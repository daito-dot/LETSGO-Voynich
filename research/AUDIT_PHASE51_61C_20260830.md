# Independent audit — Phase 51 through Phase 61C

Date: 2026-08-30

Status: **audit record, not a replacement for frozen phase results**.

This file records an independent code/reproducibility review performed after Phase61C. It exists so future sessions do not have to reconstruct audit findings from chat history or stale workspace state.

## Authority rule

- Frozen phase plans/results/reports remain the numerical authority unless an explicit superseding experiment is committed.
- Audit findings may narrow the interpretation of a result without rewriting the historical result.
- Missing historical code is reproducibility debt, not evidence that a numerical result is false.
- Post-hoc diagnostics do not retroactively change preregistered pass/fail rules; they constrain what a passed gate can legitimately be claimed to establish.

## Executive conclusion

The audit found **no reversal of the main Phase60–61 scientific sequence**:

1. the paragraph-entry effect remains real under genuine physical-leaf cross-fitting;
2. A0 remains an inadequate joint generator in the accepted record;
3. Phase61C validly passes its frozen first joint gate;
4. A1 should remain frozen and proceed to fair N/C/G comparison rather than receive an A2 post-hoc repair.

The audit does, however, require two interpretation constraints:

- exact Phase60B effect sizes/carrier rankings are not yet fully replay-certified because the historical `n=380` eligibility rule has not been recovered and the current public reimplementation contains a pooled-direction cross-fit error;
- Phase61C matches the preregistered **aggregate** line-position eta2 target, but does not reproduce the full 11-coordinate line-position profile.

## Phase 60B — cross-fit audit

### Public reimplementation issue

The current public `experiments/phase60/phase60b_feature_attribution.py` constructs a training-fold direction, but the final reported pooled result discards those fold-specific directions and recomputes a direction from the combined held-out deltas. It also computes feature standard deviations globally rather than within each training fold.

The independent audit therefore compared:

1. current pooled-held-out-direction behavior;
2. true fold-specific training direction with global scaling;
3. true fold-specific training direction with training-only scaling.

Exact source identity was checked against ZL3b/EVA Git blob SHA-1:

`2a4533ab9bdfa85db9bad602d590978953055df1`

### Corrected result on the current public parser

The current parser produces 431 paragraphs with both an eligible real transition and at least one eligible internal pseudo-boundary. Under genuine training-direction cross-fit plus training-only scaling, mean projections are:

| representation | accepted Phase60B reference | corrected cross-fit |
|---|---:|---:|
| raw EVA | 1.0072 | 0.9017 |
| conservative composites | 0.9194 | 0.7495 |
| Phase56 composites | 1.1182 | 0.9326 |

Raw-EVA fold means:

`[0.6748, 1.5039, 0.4504, 0.4852, 1.4505]`

All five physical-leaf folds remain positive. These values nearly reproduce the independently frozen Phase60A anti-circular fold values (`~0.672, 1.491, 0.467, 0.485, 1.458`), which is strong internal support for the corrected cross-fit implementation.

Training-only scaling itself has negligible effect; retaining the training-fold direction is the material correction.

### Historical eligibility remains unresolved

Accepted Phase60B reports `n=380`. The current parser gives 431 paired paragraphs, and simple sensitivity variants tested during audit did not reproduce 380. Therefore the exact historical Phase60B sample/effect sizes are **not** fully replay-certified.

A nearby `n=365` sensitivity still produced positive cross-fitted means in all representations and broadly retained the same signed carriers. Robust raw-EVA sensitivity effects included:

- TTR negative;
- mean token length negative;
- edit1-family positive;
- local previous-10 near-family positive;
- k/t mass positive;
- k-share positive;
- last-unit entropy positive.

Retained interpretation:

> The paragraph-entry transition remains robustly cross-fitted and is consistently associated with lower TTR/shorter-token structure together with increased edit1/local-near-family and k/t-related structure. Exact contribution rankings and effect sizes remain dependent on recovery of the historical Phase60B eligibility implementation.

Do **not** cite the current public Phase60B script as an exact reproduction of `phase60b_results.json` until that provenance gap is closed.

## Phase 61A — narrow mechanism demonstration, not prospective validation of strength 1.5

Phase61A evaluates the frozen strength grid `0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0` on the exposed held-out entry target. Phase61B then fixes strength `1.5` for its first joint diagnostic.

Therefore the valid claim is:

> the A0 architecture family contains a low-complexity boundary-aware entry mixture capable of reaching/exceeding the scalar entry target.

It should **not** be described as prospective validation of a preselected `strength=1.5` model on untouched held-out data. This is consistent with the existing project conclusion that 61A's narrow success did not validate A0.

## Phase 61C sensitivity — remove held-out vocabulary access

The frozen implementation shares the empirical full-manuscript token-type inventory across folds. A stricter audit rebuilt both vocabulary and edit1 neighbor graph from training physical leaves only.

This is nontrivial: roughly 48–50% of held-out token types, representing about 19–21% of held-out token occurrences, are absent from the corresponding training vocabulary.

Despite removing every held-out-only type from the generator, held-out ratios were almost unchanged:

| target | frozen 61C | training-vocabulary-only |
|---|---:|---:|
| entry projection | 0.7969 | 0.7947 |
| local-prev10 | 0.7169 | 0.7181 |
| line-position eta2 mean | 1.1163 | 1.0981 |

All selected parameter pairs were unchanged across the five folds. Full-vocabulary sharing is therefore not driving the Phase61C survival decision on the three primary targets.

> A1 simultaneously reaches the broad held-out regime for the frozen scalar entry projection, global local-prev10 level, and aggregate 11-feature line-position eta2 mean.

Not supported:

> A1 reproduces the full Voynich line-position grammar or multivariate line-position feature profile.

The non-near-family aggregate remains within `[0.5,2.0]`, so the aggregate match is not solely local-family self-feedback, but substantial cancellation is present across coordinates.

## Consequence for Phase 62 and later sessions
