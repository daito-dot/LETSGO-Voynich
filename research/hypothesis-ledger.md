## Current mechanism alternatives

Current evidence remains compatible with meaningful text plus nontrivial encoding/shorthand, hierarchical formal generation, or mixed mechanisms. Unencoded source-native structured medieval Latin is no longer jointly competitive under the frozen Phase62 S1–S3 scorecard, although it explains generic line-position S3 well.

The strongest established paragraph result remains a **manuscript-wide, transferable, short-lived paragraph-entry formal register**. Phase62B sharpens the model-family problem: ordinary structured medieval text does not reproduce the common Voynich entry direction and has much weaker local near-family excess, while generic line-position effects are easy to reproduce.

Frozen G/A1 must now be compared fairly against the already-frozen C0 reversible boundary-blind recoding family. Neither may be repaired inside Phase62C. After that, Phase62D freezes the exposed-score ranking before the sealed H62-P1 prospective profile is revealed.

## Rule for adding hypotheses

Every new entry must identify a falsification condition and distinguish structural support from semantic/decipherment support. If a hypothesis survives only after adding free exceptions, those added degrees of freedom must be recorded. Deliberate deception receives no null-result privilege.
