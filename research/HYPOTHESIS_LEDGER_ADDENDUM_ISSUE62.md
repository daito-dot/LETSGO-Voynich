## Program-level object

This phase studies the internal construction of **space-delimited Voynich tokens** under the frozen 12-slot representation. It does not establish that spaces are linguistic word boundaries, and it is not a sentence-syntax test.

See `research/TOKEN_CONSTRUCTION_PROGRAM.md` for the normative orientation of this research lane.

## H58B-CORE — one shared deep signed occupancy graph across Currier/section

Prediction:

- all four preregistered register/section contrasts satisfy the frozen `STABLE` gates;
- complete 66-edge similarity is family-wise exceptional relative to the 1,000 line-local marginal-preserving nulls;
- cross-stratum held-out transfer remains close to each target stratum's own reliability.

Observed:

- all four register/section contrasts were reliable and numerically similar;
- Pearson `R_full` ranged from `0.6995` to `0.9613`;
- directional held-out transfer ranged from `0.6723` to `0.9460`;
- but no contrast had `p_maxT <= .01`;
- the null maxT distribution itself was extremely high: median `0.94939`, 95th percentile `0.96323`.

Frozen global classification:

> `CURRIER/SECTION GRAPH STABILITY INCONCLUSIVE`

Status: **NOT ESTABLISHED / INCONCLUSIVE**.

Permitted interpretation: the strata look strongly similar numerically, but the complete-graph similarity is not stronger than the resemblance generated by lower-order line-local occupancy architecture under the frozen null.

## H58B-MIX — pooled #58A graph is materially generated by register/section mixture

Frozen difference evidence required at least one reliable contrast with either `R_full < .40` or directional transfer `< .30`.

Observed:

- no register/section contrast met a practical difference gate;
- no directional transfer was below `.30`;
- the weakest `R_full` was `0.6995`.

Status: **NOT SUPPORTED**.

The failure to establish shared-core stability must not be relabeled as evidence of mixture.

## H58B-POS — line position materially changes the graph

Observed:

- all three position comparisons were highly reliable;
- `R_full = 0.8682–0.9431`;
- directional transfer remained `0.7866–0.9360`;
- none was family-wise exceptional against the same maxT null;
- none approached the frozen difference gates.

Frozen position classification:

> `LINE-POSITION GRAPH STABILITY INCONCLUSIVE`

Status: **INCONCLUSIVE; MATERIAL POSITION DIFFERENCE NOT SUPPORTED**.

## Retained structural result

Issue #58B does not erase #58A.

Retain:

- space-delimited tokens are strongly constrained under the 12-slot occupancy representation;
- the complete graph shows reproducible numerical structure within strata;
- parser `max` sensitivity continues to show high observed graph resemblance;
- raw whole-graph resemblance is strongly contaminated by structure retained under the line-local marginal-preserving null.

The new constraint is methodological and scientific:

> **A raw 66-edge graph correlation is not a sufficiently discriminating measure of a shared deep token-construction mechanism.**

## New follow-up hypothesis generated after #58B

The next phase may test a new, separately preregistered hypothesis:

> After estimating and removing each stratum/edge's null-expected contribution from line-local slot prevalence, a graph-level residual interaction signal exists and its signed pattern transfers across manuscript strata.

This hypothesis is **generated by** the #58B failure mode and was not part of the #58B frozen confirmatory claim.

Required protection against circularity:

1. freeze an edge-wise null-centering/standardization rule before new target reveal;
2. use separate deterministic reference-null and test-null sets so the residual representation is not estimated and validated on the same null realizations;
3. test residual existence before interpreting residual cross-stratum similarity;
4. retain all 66 edges rather than selecting #58A/#58B leaders;
5. preserve the same external strata unless a new source audit independently justifies a change;
6. control multiplicity prospectively;
7. keep semantic/cipher/plaintext interpretation outside this stage.
